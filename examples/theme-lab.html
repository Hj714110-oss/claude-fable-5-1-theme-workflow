Section 01 · Method

[01]Scope & method

The Tidewater Archive is an invented institution created for this style study. Its sole purpose is to give the three colour states — Noon, Night, and Morning — something honest to hold: a date line, a numbered index with dot leaders, an asymmetric title, a narrow reading column, and one restrained evidence frame.

Switching modes changes the hero field, the artwork, the accent, and the mood of the page. It never changes the information architecture, so the three readings can be compared like prints from the same negative.

Section 02 · Modes

[02]Three readings

Noon reads as a public record: open, calm, scan-friendly. Night reads as the technical annex: systems, risk, and things checked after dark. Morning reads as the archive proper: dates, paper, and the people who filed the documents.

None of the three is a reskin. Each keeps the same skeleton — header, hero, index, reading column, evidence — and only re-tunes the tokens that carry mood.
